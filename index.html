LifeLine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026.

Provider and contact

LifeLine is provided by MVS Digital Software. Privacy and support contact: mvsdigitalsoftware@gmail.com. The application is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

Processing on your device

LifeLine processes activities, notes, dates, scheduling rules, completion information, tracked sessions, active-timer state, settings, language and holiday preferences, playlists and media references locally. Local audio scanning reads media information needed to display and play your files. You choose photos through an Android picker or file provider. The provider may offer cloud-backed items under its own policies. LifeLine processes the selected bytes locally and stores private copies of artwork, edit documents and recoverable drafts. The app does not require an account.

The offline build removes remote weather, remote availability checks, device-location access, advertising, consent SDKs and billing SDKs. Its Android manifest does not grant internet access, and its WebView blocks network loads. No LifeLine telemetry or analytics transmission was identified in the audited application code. These statements describe this application build, not the independent behavior of Android, Google Play, other applications or your email provider.

Old weather/location preference values from an earlier installation may remain locally to avoid destructive migration. This version does not request new device location or send those saved values to a server.

Storage and protection

App records are stored in local WebView storage, native preferences and, where applicable, a local browser audio database. Original media may reside in shared device storage. No separate app-level encryption of records or JSON exports has been verified or promised. Device-level protection depends on your operating system, screen lock and device configuration. Protect your device and exported files.

Android cloud backup is disabled in the application configuration. Device-transfer behavior depends on Android/OEM handling; the existing device-transfer rules have not been verified on a device. Do not rely on transfer to preserve media permissions or every record.

Permissions

Audio/media permissions allow local music access; older Android versions use legacy storage permissions for supported media operations. Notification and exact-alarm access support reminders. Foreground-service and wake-lock capabilities support playback and scheduling. You can manage optional permissions in Android Settings. This build does not request internet, advertising-identifier or device-location permissions.

Backups, sharing and deletion

The JSON export contains activities and recorded sessions, which may include personal notes. It does not contain cover artwork or editor drafts. It is not a complete backup of settings, playlists, audio files or the active timer. Exported backups are separate files under your control. Import validates and adds supported records; repeated import may duplicate them.

When you choose to share media or a backup, the selected destination app controls what happens next and may upload it. The provider does not receive that content merely because it is exported. Keep backups in a location you trust.

App records remain until removed through available app controls or by clearing application storage/uninstalling. Deleting an activity does not necessarily delete historical recorded sessions. Removing a cover from a song detaches the cover. Private copies and temporary files may persist in app storage until cleared by Android or when you clear app data. Exported backups, externally shared copies and original audio files must be managed separately. Deleting playlist references is different from deleting original media. Existing installations are not automatically wiped by this update.

Support communications and third parties

If you email support, you voluntarily send the message, your email address and any attachments to the support mailbox through your email provider and Google's Gmail service. Avoid sending private backups or sensitive information unless needed. Support correspondence may be retained to address the request and relevant obligations; no fixed retention period is promised by this policy. You may request deletion or correction through the contact above, subject to applicable obligations and technical limits. The provider cannot remotely inspect, recover or erase app records held only on your device.

Google Play manages distribution and may process information independently under its own policies. The planned release covers all available Google Play markets except Thailand and Israel. Google Play applies store availability using account-country settings; LifeLine does not collect location to enforce them.

Changes and rights

Changes to this disclosure will be supplied with an app update and an effective date. Offline installations do not automatically receive updated documents. Contact the provider about privacy concerns or requests involving support communications. This disclosure does not limit rights available under applicable law.
